What the 99% really measures.

The training data has a shortcut: nearly every real article is a Reuters wire story. A single rule that only looks for “(Reuters)” beats the model. So this version strips publisher cues before training and before every prediction, and it is tested on claims from a different source.

How it works.

Clean

Datelines, image credits, handles and links are removed so the model can't identify the outlet.

Weigh words

TF-IDF over words and word pairs feeds a logistic regression. Each word's push is its weight in your text times its learned coefficient.

Place the topic

The same features, reduced with PCA, are matched to one of six k-means topic clusters learned from 39,000 articles.
